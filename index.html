Categories

Education Support

Support meritorious but needy Students to pursue their dreams. Poor students should not drop out of Education because of weak financial condition.

Know More

Medical Treatment

Support needy patients for life-threatening illness/condition/disease. No one should suffer gravely for lack of financial support in their medical treatment.

Know More

Livelihood Support

‘Support individuals (preferably Women & Old people) with initial capital to start a small source of Livelihood for themselves. This assistance is provided mainly focusing on people from Rural areas.

Know More

Disaster Relief

Support those who are unfortunately affected by natural calamities & lost their near ones, homes or livelihoods. Help them to return to normal life by helping in their rehabilitation.

Know More

Orphan Support

Support the educational and other expenses of needy orphan children so that the child is not deprived of even the basic needs for want of a guardian.

Know More

Higher Education Scholarship

Support those students who have scored well in X & XII and got admission in Medical, Engineering or other Professional courses, but do not have required financial funding in the family.

IndiaZakat.com is India's 1st Zakat-based Crowdfunding platform initiated by the Association of Muslim Professionals (AMP), with a view to bringing the Zakat Seekers and Zakat Givers on a single platform. Its objective is to bring a positive socio-economic transformation in the lives of the underprivileged people, without seeking to generate any commercial benefits.
